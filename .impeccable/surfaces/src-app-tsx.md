# Frontend direction

Scope: the existing local game, from setup through reveal. Mode: Operate.
The user chose playful arcade and direct implementation in the app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A small party game that feels ready to play. Scene selection and two
auditions are the main objects, with technical notes subordinate to the next action.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-purple cabinet, warm cream content, flat lavender controls. Bungee
display lettering, Barlow UI text, rounded substantial controls, crisp SVG icons.
No neon glows, fake waveforms, or simulated audio data.

STORY: Activate sound, choose a scene, privately direct one line, hand off, listen,
choose, reveal. Existing rules and authored copy stay intact. Listener privacy wins
over decorative continuity.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact brand/audio bar; large stacked arcade premise at left,
clear sound activation and player fields beneath; scene list at right with cream
surfaces, bold titles, readiness and one unambiguous choice per scene. Mobile
stacks setup before scene choices. The directing desk uses a script column and an
audition rail; selecting a take gives it a solid selected header and check mark.

FORM: User-pinned arcade selection screen. Seed 40688c9d assigned candidate 4;
the explicit arcade selection takes priority over unrelated catalog worlds.
The useful discipline of the exposure-record challenger is explicit state without
relying on color; of ticket wallets, visible remaining attempts; of typographic
tiles, an unmistakable task hierarchy. Their visual worlds are declined because
they dilute the user's arcade choice. Signature motion: one brief reveal stamp;
all other feedback is immediate, with reduced-motion support.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Verification: built app, prepared audio only, desktop/mobile/intermediate widths,
keyboard focus, long names, offline font assets, and the full listener privacy path.
