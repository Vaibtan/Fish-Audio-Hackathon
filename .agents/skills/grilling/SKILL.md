# Stress-test a decision

Establish which outcome the user wants to decide. Read available facts yourself;
reserve questions for preferences, tradeoffs and missing information that could
materially change the result.

Ask a small round of independent questions, with your recommendation and its
consequences. Keep dependent questions for after their prerequisites are settled.
Use neutral wording that lets the user disagree; do not shape every question so
that a simple yes accepts the entire proposal.

Challenge assumptions with concrete failure scenarios and update the proposal as
answers arrive. Stop when the requested decision is supported and remaining
uncertainty is explicit, rather than exhausting every possible branch.

For a requested interview that also records terminology and decisions, consult
[domain-modeling](../domain-modeling/SKILL.md). Record settled decisions within
scope. A discussion does not authorize implementing a newly proposed design;
continue implementation when that authority was already given.
